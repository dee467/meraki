About Hannah

Hi, I'm Hannah.

After years of living with back pain, it came to a head when training for the London Marathon — my back completely gave up on me. That's when I found Pilates, and it changed everything. What started as a way to feel better quickly became a passion, and now, ten years on, I love helping others discover the power of Pilates too (and I'm still running too!).

Mum of two (or three, if you count my very needy Vizsla), lover of all things outdoors — beautiful views and the shimmer of sun on water. I truly believe Pilates keeps you doing what you love, whatever that looks like for you.

That's why I set up Meraki Pilates in Wotton-under-Edge, offering both mat and reformer classes, all designed for a mix of abilities, relaxed and fun, and open to everyone. No experience needed, no intimidating atmosphere. Just good movement and good people.

  • Relaxed, welcoming classes for every ability
  • No experience or equipment needed
  • Small groups on the reformer, max 4 people
  • A community that shows up for each other
